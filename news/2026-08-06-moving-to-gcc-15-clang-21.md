# Moving to GCC 15 and Clang 21 as default compiler versions

As part of our regular toolchain updates, we're planning to update the default
versions of GCC (for linux) to v15 and of Clang (for OSX) to v21, in one week.

For more details, see https://github.com/conda-forge/conda-forge-pinning-feedstock/pull/8795.
