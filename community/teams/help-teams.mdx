# Help teams

As per the [governance](../governance.md), the conda-forge community is organized in several teams.
Most help teams belong to [`staged-recipes`](./dynamic-subteam/staged-recipes.md),
but there are some that are concerned with new architectures or other migrations.
You can ping them in your issues and pull requests (e.g. `@conda-forge/help-python`).
The list below is automatically generated from the metadata collected in
[`conda-forge/governance`](https://github.com/conda-forge/governance/tree/main/teams).
