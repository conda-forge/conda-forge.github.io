# How to build on GPU runners

By default, provisioned CI machines do not feature a GPU. See [how to configure self-hosted runners](/docs/how-to/advanced/self-hosted-runners/) if your feedstock needs access to additional resources like GPUs or long-running builds, and [how to distinguish CPU and GPU jobs](/docs/how-to/advanced/self-hosted-runners/#distinguishing-cpu-vs-gpu-jobs) when your recipe builds both.
